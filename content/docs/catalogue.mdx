title: "Products and prices"
description: "What you sell, and what it costs, once or every period."
status: experimental
---

A **product** is what you sell. A **price** is an amount for it, either once or on a schedule.

```ts
const product = await cambill.products.create({ name: "Hosting", external_id: "sku_hosting" })

const once = await cambill.prices.create({ product: product.id, currency: "USD", unit_amount: 1000 })
const monthly = await cambill.prices.create({
  product: product.id,
  currency: "USD",
  unit_amount: 900,
  type: "recurring",
  interval: "month", // day | week | month | year
  interval_count: 1,
})
```

## A price never changes

Its amount and currency are fixed. To charge more, create a new price and archive the old one. That is what keeps old invoices and running subscriptions true.

```ts
await cambill.prices.update(oldPrice.id, { active: false })
```

You can still rename a price (`nickname`) and change its `metadata`. Sending a different `unit_amount` answers `400 Price Is Fixed`.

## Use a price on an invoice

```ts
lines: [{ price: once.id, quantity: 3 }] // amount and description come from the catalogue
```

The product's name becomes the line's description unless you give one. The price's currency must match the invoice's.

## Archive a product

`products.update(id, { name, active: false })`. Archived products and prices cannot be added to new invoices or subscriptions.

## Pricing when you create a product

The product form takes prices too: add one or more (one time or recurring) and they are created with the product. To change an existing product's name, description or external ID, use **Edit** on its page. A price's amount never changes: add a new price and archive the old one.

## Features are separate

Products, coupons, subscriptions and finance reports are each switched on for a business on their own. Turning on products does not turn on coupons or subscriptions; ask CamBill to enable the ones you need. Subscriptions also need invoicing, customers and products.
