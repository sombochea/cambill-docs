## Test and live

Every business has two modes. **The key decides the mode**: `khpay_test_…` always works in test mode, `khpay_live_…` in live. They never mix: a test key cannot read a live object, and test mode never moves real money. Test mode has the same features as live, plus a way to simulate a payment.

## Keys

A key is a secret. Send it in the `X-API-Key` header, from your server only. Rotate it in the dashboard; the old key stops working at once. A request with an unknown key answers `401`.

## Amounts and times

- **Money is an integer in the currency's smallest unit.** USD in cents (`1050` is $10.50), KHR in riel (`4000` is ៛4,000). Never a decimal.
- **Currencies:** `USD` and `KHR`.
- **Times** are RFC 3339 in UTC, like `2026-12-01T09:30:00Z`.

## Objects and ids

Ids are readable: `cus_test_…`, `prod_…`, `price_…`, `inv_…`, `sub_…`, `pi_…`. The mode is in the id.

| Object | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Payment intent | One payment: an amount, a KHQR, a status. |
| Customer | Someone you bill. Has your own `external_id`. |
| Product, price | What you sell, and what it costs, once or every period. |
| Coupon, tax rate | A discount; a tax. |
| Invoice | A numbered bill your customer pays from a link. |
| Subscription | Bills a customer for recurring prices, one invoice a period. |

## Money never moves through CamBill

Your customer's bank settles to your own account through your connector. CamBill watches for the payment, marks the invoice paid and tells you. That is why invoices must match exactly: a payment that does not equal the invoice is not applied.
