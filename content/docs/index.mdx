CamBill lets you get paid in Cambodia without building a billing system. You make a payment or an invoice with one API call; your customer pays with any KHQR banking app; CamBill tells you when the money arrives.

## What you can build

- **One-off payments.** A payment intent gives you a KHQR code to show, a hosted checkout, and a status to read.
- **Invoices.** Itemised, numbered, with tax and discounts. Your customer gets a page to pay and a PDF to keep.
- **A catalogue.** Products with one-time and recurring prices.
- **Subscriptions.** Each period becomes an invoice your customer pays from a link. No card is stored.

## How it works

You call the API from your server with a secret key. CamBill never holds the money: your customer's bank settles straight to your own account through the connector you set up. Test mode is a full copy of the product with a "simulate payment" button, so you can build the whole flow without moving money.
