---
title: "Going live"
description: "What changes when you use a live key."
---

1. **Connect your real account.** In **Connectors**, set up Bakong KHQR (or ABA) for **live** mode.
2. **Get verified.** Live invoicing needs a business a CamBill admin has reviewed: in **Settings → Business → Verification**, choose **Business** (a registered company or shop: legal name, registration number, tax ID, country, address, contact email and phone) or **Individual** (a sole person: full name, country, address, contact email and phone, no registration or tax papers), then press **Request verification** (you can add a note about what you sell). The button stays off until every detail is saved. Payments do not need it.
3. **Switch the key** to `khpay_live_…` and the webhook endpoint to your production URL.
4. **Know the limits.** Live mode limits one invoice's size, how many you issue in 24 hours and how many are open at once. A refused request says which.
5. **Handle failures.** Retry with an `Idempotency-Key` (the SDK does). On `429`, wait `Retry-After`.

## Keep it safe

- Keep keys and the webhook secret on your server and out of your repository.
- Verify every webhook signature.
- Do not show customers anything you have not read back from the API.

CamBill logs every change with who made it (a person, an API key, a payer or the system). Admins can export it from **Audit logs**. CamBill does not hold your money: your bank settles to you.
