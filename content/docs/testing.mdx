---
title: "Testing"
description: "Test mode, sample data, and a clock you can move."
---

- **Test keys** (`khpay_test_…`) work in test mode only. Nothing moves real money.
- **Simulate a payment** with the button on any test checkout. This is how you test `payment_intent.succeeded` and `invoice.paid`.
- **Sample data:** the dashboard's **Add sample data** (or `POST /v1/businesses/:id/sandbox/seed`) creates a customer, a product with a one-time and a monthly price, a tax rate and an issued invoice. Run it again for another invoice.
- **Move the clock:** `subscriptions.advance(id)` bills the next period now.
- **Retry safely:** send the same `Idempotency-Key` twice and you get the first response back.

Test mode has no invoice limits and does not need your business to be verified.
